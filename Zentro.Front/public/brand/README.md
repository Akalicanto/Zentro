## Prompts utilizados

Símbolo, imagen nueva con transparencia:

> Use case: logo / brand identity. Create one production-ready app emblem for Zentro, a refined personal finance app. Asset type: square standalone brand mark, used in a sidebar and as a tiny favicon. Transparent background. Subject: an original beautiful geometric letter Z composed of two continuous rounded folded bands that subtly suggest balance and a gentle ascending path. Sophisticated minimalist flat graphic, clean precise silhouette, generous negative space, violet pastel and deep plum with a tiny warm cream highlight. Clear at small sizes. Center one large emblem occupying 85 percent of the canvas. No words, no letters other than the abstract Z, no additional icon, no text, no border, no mockup, no grain, no photorealism, no 3D shadow. Palette aligned with pastel violet #B69BDD, plum #70529E and warm cream #FFF8ED. True transparent alpha around the emblem. One single symbol only, elegant memorable proportions.

Logo completo, edición del símbolo anterior con transparencia:

> Use case: logo brand identity. Edit the supplied Zentro emblem into a complete horizontal brand logo lockup. Preserve the recognizable folded rounded Z shape and exact violet, plum, warm cream colour identity. Make a polished minimal horizontal composition: compact emblem on the left, the exact lowercase word "zentro" on the right in a custom refined rounded geometric sans-serif, bold but elegant, dark plum #423354. Exact spelling z e n t r o, with no punctuation. Wide horizontal canvas approximately 3:1, transparent background, logo fills the usable width with balanced breathing room. The emblem must remain simple and crisp; make the wordmark large and beautifully kerned. No extra words, no tagline, no frame, no mockup, no watermark. True transparent alpha.
